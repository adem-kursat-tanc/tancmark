# Code of conduct

Participate with respect, technical honesty, and care for user privacy. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate deception, credential sharing, doxxing, and publication of private test media are not accepted.

Disagreements should be resolved from reproducible evidence. Do not pressure maintainers to weaken security gates or describe partial evidence as ownership.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel or GitHub's private security-advisory workflow when privacy or safety is involved. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation to protect the project and its users.
